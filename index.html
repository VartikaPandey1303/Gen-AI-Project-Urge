The problem

Your wallet leaks thousands a month through purchases you never paused to question.

Zero-friction buying

Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy - one tap, no pause, money gone. The UX is designed to outrun your judgment.

No running tally

Your brain can't track 30+ micro-transactions a month. By the time you add them up, it's already too late.

Trackers are autopsies

Expense trackers tell you where money went after it's gone. You need a checkpoint before you pay, not a report after.

Based on a survey of 40 working professionals aged 22–28 across Bangalore, Delhi and Mumbai. Average self-reported impulse spending: ₹3,200–4,800/month, primarily on food delivery and quick commerce.
